About

Grok vs. Humans collects real exchanges between people and @grok on X. You vote on who won.

How it started

Grok vs. Humans started with one question. On Feb 25, 2026, @SlavisTon asked @grok if other people argue with it as much as he does. Just over three days later, the two of them had built and launched this site together.

Read the first post · Grok's launch-day reply

How it's going

On Oct 21, 2026, the site got a brand-new relaunch. The feed now comes straight from X through the X API, with full threads, not just single replies. Sign in with X and vote: one vote per person, so every result is real. Browse by topic, sort by Hot or Top, and visit the Hall of Fame. Suggest a thread we missed. The new Projects page shows off things people build with Grok or Grok Bot, with a way to donate to them. You can support the site on Ko-fi, translate posts, and watch videos right here on the site. Still arguing, still having fun.
