2. What we use and what it collects

  • Hosting (Cloudflare Pages): The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics (measurement ID G-M58E7KLM56): We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. It sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) and collects usage data like pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type, and referring site. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We use these statistics only to improve the site. You can block it with your browser's cookie or tracker settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Voting (Firebase Realtime Database, Google): When you vote on a battle, only the anonymous vote total for that battle is increased (for example, “Grok: 12, Human: 9”). We do not store who voted. Your browser connects to Google's Firebase servers to read and update these totals, so Google processes your IP address as part of that connection.
  • Local storage in your browser: To stop the same browser from voting twice, the site saves a small flag (for example voted_battle_21) in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Submissions (Formspree): If you use the Submit form, the X thread link, your email address and your optional message are sent to us through Formspree, a form-handling service. We use your email only to reply about your submission. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Third-party content and libraries: Pages load fonts, icons and scripts from content delivery networks (Google Fonts, cdnjs, Tailwind CDN, Google's gstatic) and show images and videos hosted elsewhere (for example X/Twitter, catbox.moe, Tenor, Giphy). Your browser connects to these services directly, so they receive your IP address and standard browser information.
  • Support link (Ko-fi): If a “Support this project” link is shown, it takes you to Ko-fi. The site loads nothing from Ko-fi until you click it, and any tip is handled entirely by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy.

3. What we don't do

There are no user accounts, no advertising, and no selling or renting of personal information. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. How long data is kept

Vote totals are kept for as long as the site exists. Submission emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the submission. Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in Google Analytics. Cloudflare and the other providers above keep their logs according to their own policies.

5. Visitors in the EU, EEA and UK

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the site (hosting, analytics, vote counting), and on your request when you send a submission. Google, Cloudflare and Formspree may process data in the United States. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. To use any of these rights, email us.

6. Visitors in California and other US states

Some US state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, give residents the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email us. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
