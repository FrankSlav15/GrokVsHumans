Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026 (updated: Google Analytics replaced by cookieless Cloudflare Web Analytics; new contact address)

GrokVsHumans (grokvshumans.com) is a private, non-commercial fan project run by one person in the United States. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the site and why. We do not sell your personal information and we do not run ads.

Travis Johnson, Iowa, USA

2. What we use and what it collects

  • Hosting (Cloudflare Pages): The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless): We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and does not fingerprint you or track you across sites. When a page loads, a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com reports the page address, the referring page, your browser and device type, your country (derived by Cloudflare from your IP address, which is not stored for analytics) and page-load timings. We only see aggregated statistics. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Sign in with X (X Corp.): Voting requires signing in with your X account; browsing the site does not. When you sign in, X asks you to authorize this site with the read-only permissions tweet.read and users.read. We receive your numeric X user ID, @handle, display name, profile picture URL and verified status, and we store them in our database to show your identity on the site and to tie your votes to your account. We do not receive your password, we cannot post or act on your behalf, and we discard the access token X gives us right after reading your profile. You can revoke access at any time in your X settings under Connected apps. Your use of X is governed by X's privacy policy.
  • Votes and session (Cloudflare D1): Each vote is stored with your X user ID so that each account can vote once per battle or post (you may change your vote). Vote totals are public; who voted for what is not shown to other visitors. After you sign in we set one strictly necessary, secure, HttpOnly session cookie (__Host-gvh_sess, valid for 30 days or until you sign out). Earlier anonymous vote totals (from before sign-in existed) were carried over without any personal data.
  • Comments, likes/dislikes and reports (Cloudflare D1): If you comment, we store the text, the time, the battle, meme or post it belongs to and your X user ID. Comments are public and are shown with your @handle, name and profile picture. Likes and dislikes (on battles, memes and comments) are stored with your X user ID; only the totals are shown to others. If you report a comment, we store the report, your X user ID and your optional reason; reports are visible only to the site's moderator. We use this data only to run these features, enforce the comment rules (an automatic word filter, a limit of 5 comments per 10 minutes, auto-hiding after several reports) and moderate the site. You can delete your own comments at any time with the “Delete” button, and remove a reaction by tapping it again. Deleted comments are removed from our database immediately.
  • Public X posts shown on the site: The feed shows public exchanges between people and @grok from X. We copy the public post text, the author's @handle, display name, profile picture URL, verified status, post time and links to attached media from X's official API, and we store them in our database (Cloudflare D1) so they can be shown and voted on. Every exchange is reviewed by a person before it appears. We check shown posts against X about once a day and remove an exchange from the site when a post in it has been deleted or made private on X. On the feed, each post is shown as X's official embedded post: your browser loads X's widget script from platform.twitter.com (only when a post scrolls into view) and the post itself from X, so X receives your IP address and browser information and may set its own cookies under X's privacy policy. We ask X not to use this for personalisation (do-not-track setting on every embed). If you are the author of a post shown here and want it removed, email us or reply to @SlavisTon and we will remove it, normally within 24 hours; we can also exclude your account from future selection.
  • Submissions (Formspree): If you use the Submit form, the X thread link, your email address and your optional message are sent to us through Formspree, a form-handling service. We use your email only to reply about your submission. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Third-party content and libraries: Pages load fonts, icons and scripts from content delivery networks (Google Fonts, cdnjs, Tailwind CDN, Google's gstatic) and show images and videos hosted elsewhere (for example X/Twitter, catbox.moe, Tenor, Giphy). Your browser connects to these services directly, so they receive your IP address and standard browser information.
  • Support link (Ko-fi): If a “Support this project” link is shown, it takes you to Ko-fi. The site loads nothing from Ko-fi until you click it, and any tip is handled entirely by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy.

3. What we don't do

There is no advertising, and no selling or renting of personal information. The only account is your own X login; we do not create separate passwords. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.

4. How long data is kept

Your votes, comments, reactions and reports are kept for as long as the site exists, or until you delete them (comments, reactions) or ask us to delete your account data. Moderators may remove comments and reports at any time. Sessions expire after 30 days. Submission emails are kept only as long as needed to handle the submission. Aggregated analytics data is kept by Cloudflare Web Analytics according to Cloudflare's retention limits. Cloudflare and the other providers above keep their logs according to their own policies.

5. Visitors in the EU, EEA and UK

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the site (hosting, analytics, vote counting, preventing duplicate votes), and on your request when you send a submission. X, Cloudflare, Formspree and the content delivery networks listed above may process data in the United States. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. To use any of these rights, including deleting your account and votes, email us.

6. Visitors in California and other US states

Some US state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, give residents the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email us. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. Signing in, voting and commenting are only for people 13 or older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
